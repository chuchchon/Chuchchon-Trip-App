# Chuchchon-Trip-App

- `/` — Hokkaido Momiji Drive (18–24 Oct 2026), the trip app used on the road.
- `/planner/` — Trip Planner: the same app for any trip and any country (several countries and time zones in one trip). Plan in Excel or Google Sheets (template with a Thai guide and an AI guide inside), upload the file, or build a plan from the photos of a trip. Each trip keeps its own plan, photos and Tripbook on the phone.
